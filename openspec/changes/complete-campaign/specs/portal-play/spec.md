## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete campaign evenings
Each of the 24 doors SHALL provide a distinct approximately twenty-minute first-person mission, two or three signature mechanics, a solo solution, a co-op solution and a keepsake delivered to the shared hall.

#### Scenario: First clear on an eligible date
- **GIVEN** a player has missed every earlier day
- **WHEN** they enter the current eligible door and complete its objectives
- **THEN** they can extract its keepsake and understand that evening's story independently

#### Scenario: A late finale
- **WHEN** a new party opens the final door after December 24
- **THEN** it can complete the ending without collecting the previous 23 keepsakes
